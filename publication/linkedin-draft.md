# LinkedIn draft

I built a demand forecasting and inventory optimization portfolio demo in Python.

It takes SKU sales through validation, daily demand aggregation, time-aware forecasting evaluation, and replenishment calculations (safety stock, reorder point, and EOQ). I also added a Streamlit dashboard, a FastAPI demo endpoint, SQL examples, and automated tests.

Important caveat: the included data is synthetic. The project demonstrates an engineering and analytical workflow; it does not claim real-world forecast accuracy, cost savings, or purchasing results.

The next step is to evaluate the approach on appropriately licensed data with operations stakeholders.
